Ideas in Action

Sample Projects & Marketing Concepts

Illustrative examples of how digital marketing strategies can be planned for different types of businesses.

Concept · SEO

Local Business SEO Strategy

Objective: Help a hypothetical local business improve its visibility in relevant search results.

Approach: Review search intent, improve page titles and headings, develop useful service content and identify relevant local SEO opportunities.

Evaluation: Search impressions, organic clicks and relevant enquiries.
Concept · Website

Small Business Website Refresh

Objective: Make a hypothetical business website clearer and easier to navigate.

Approach: Organize service information, improve mobile layouts, clarify calls to action and make contact details easier to find.

Evaluation: Contact clicks, enquiry submissions and mobile usability.

Social Media Content Plan

Objective: Help a hypothetical local brand communicate consistently with its audience.

Approach: Develop content themes, educational post ideas, captions and a publishing calendar aligned with business goals.

Evaluation: Reach, engagement, profile visits and relevant website traffic.
